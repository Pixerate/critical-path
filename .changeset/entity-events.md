Domain events for every team, container and iteration change, and collision-safe ids.

- New events: `team.updated`, `team.deleted`, `container.updated`, `container.deleted`, `iteration.created`, `iteration.updated` and `iteration.deleted`. They are delivered to webhooks like other events; deleted events carry the ids needed to route them to the right tenant. Previously these changes published nothing.
- Event ids and the ids generated by `InMemoryStore`, `SQLiteStore` and domain entities now use `crypto.randomUUID()` (e.g. `task_<uuid>`) instead of 7 random characters, which were likely to collide at around a million records.
